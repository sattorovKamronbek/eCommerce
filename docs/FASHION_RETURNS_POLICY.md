# Clothing returns: proposed operating policy

The owner asked us to compare stores and derive one common rule (18 November 2026). This is a draft for the Uzbekistan launch; it is not yet an implemented returns process or a substitute for review against applicable consumer law.

- For eligible non-defective clothing, request a return within 10 calendar days after receipt. Individual items may be returned. The item must be unused, undamaged, with its labels and packaging and reasonable proof of purchase. Legally excluded categories are subject to the applicable exceptions.
- The buyer pays return shipping for a change-of-mind return. The seller pays reasonable return shipping for a defective or incorrect item.
- Defective or misdescribed items are handled under applicable statutory rights; the 10-day change-of-mind rule does not reduce those rights. Where a refund is due, use the original payment method where practicable and comply with applicable law and provider requirements.
- Sellers set outbound delivery prices. The precise return contact, address, inspection, refund handling, exceptions and dispute resolution must be established before launch; no automated return or refund currently exists.

## Xaridor uchun qoida loyihasi

Yaroqli va nuqsonsiz kiyimni olgan kundan boshlab 10 kalendar kun ichida qaytarish so‘rovini berishingiz mumkin. Mahsulot ishlatilmagan, shikastlanmagan, yorlig‘i va qadoqlanishi saqlangan bo‘lishi hamda xaridni tasdiqlovchi ma’lumot taqdim etilishi kerak. Buyurtmadagi alohida mahsulotni ham qaytarish mumkin. Qonunda qaytarilishi cheklangan mahsulotlarga tegishli istisnolar qo‘llanadi.

Fikringiz o‘zgargani sababli qaytarishda qaytarib yuborish xarajatini xaridor, nuqsonli yoki noto‘g‘ri mahsulot yuborilganda esa sotuvchi to‘laydi. Nuqsonli yoki tavsifga mos kelmaydigan tovarlar bo‘yicha qonuniy huquqlaringiz 10 kunlik ushbu shart bilan cheklanmaydi. Pul qaytarilishi lozim bo‘lsa, qonun va to‘lov provayderi shartlariga muvofiq imkon qadar dastlabki to‘lov usuli qo‘llanadi.

Ushbu matn loyiha: aloqa, qaytarish manzili, ko‘rib chiqish va to‘lovni qaytarish jarayoni ishga tushirishdan oldin belgilanadi.

Basis: [Uzbekistan consumer protection law](https://lex.uz/docs/-4704), especially articles 13–18; [Uzum FAQ](https://uzum.uz/uz/faq); [Asaxiy rules](https://asaxiy.uz/page/rules); [Olcha returns](https://olcha.uz/oz/page/return-and-exchange). The 10-day period after receipt is the proposed store policy, not a claim that every statutory period begins on delivery. Other jurisdictions require separate review.
